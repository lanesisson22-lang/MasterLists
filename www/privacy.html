Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026

List is a to-do list app. It is built so that your information stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. List has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send your lists, tasks or any other information to us or to anyone else.

Where your data is stored

The lists and tasks you create are saved only on your device: in the app's private storage on iPhone, iPad and Android, or in your browser's local storage on the web. If you delete the app or clear your browser data, they are deleted too.

Sharing

If you tap Share list, the list's text is handed to your device's share sheet, and you choose where it goes. List does not see or keep what you share.

Permissions

List does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos. It uses vibration for small haptic taps only.

Children

List collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the support contact shown on List's App Store or Google Play listing.
